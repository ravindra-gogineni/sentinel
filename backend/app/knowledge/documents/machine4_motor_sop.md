# Machine 4 Motor Housing Maintenance Procedure
DEMO / APPROVED FOR HACKATHON SIMULATION

Before performing maintenance on the Motor Housing of Machine 4:
1. Isolate power supply and confirm the unit is completely stopped.
2. Apply Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure LOTO-M4-005 to the primary circuit breaker.
3. Loosen the retention bolts on the motor housing assembly using the approved tool specification.
4. Inspect the drive belt for wear or tension loss.

# Machine 7 Operating & Maintenance Manual
DEMO / APPROVED FOR HACKATHON SIMULATION

Machine 7 is an automated hydraulic pressing station.
Primary hydraulic drive operates at 180 Bar system pressure.
Regular inspection of hydraulic fluid levels and seals is mandatory every 250 operating hours.
Use a 19 mm socket driver for main drive housing access cover bolts.

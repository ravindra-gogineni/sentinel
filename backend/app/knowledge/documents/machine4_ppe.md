# Machine 4 Mandatory PPE Requirements
DEMO / APPROVED FOR HACKATHON SIMULATION

Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for operating or maintaining Machine 4:
1. Heavy-duty safety gloves (ANSI Level 4 cut resistance).
2. Impact-resistant eye protection (ANSI Z87.1 approved safety glasses with side shields).
3. Steel-toe work boots.
4. Hearing protection (noise reduction rating of 25 dB or higher during operation).
